## My Favorite Series: Attack on Titan

A dark fantasy anime about humanity fighting for survival behind giant walls, while hiding a much bigger secret about the world outside.

**Why I like it:**
- Big plot twists that change how you see earlier seasons
- Strong character growth, especially for Eren and Armin
- Incredible animation in the final season

> "If you don't fight, you can't win." — Eren Yeager
